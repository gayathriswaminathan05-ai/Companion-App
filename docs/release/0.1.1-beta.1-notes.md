# Companion 0.1.1-beta.1

An early Mac beta: a little desktop plant for tasks, gentle breaks, guided breathing and jokes.

**Download `Companion-0.1.1-beta.1-arm64.dmg` from Assets below.** The source-code ZIP is for developers, not the app installer.

- Apple-chip Macs (M1 or newer), macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Other macOS versions/hardware still need testing; Intel Macs, Windows and phones are not supported by this installer.
- This build is not signed with Apple Developer ID or notarized. macOS may block it initially. Only install if you trust this source. Follow the included guide and Apple's [opening instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445). If macOS reports damage or malware, stop and contact the beta inviter; do not disable security globally.
- No AI chat, cloud voice or Gmail alerts. No API key or AI subscription required.
- Tasks, reminders, speech recognition and speaking run on your Mac. Optional Google Calendar connects directly to Google for read-only alerts.

## Try it

1. Download and open **Companion-0.1.1-beta.1-arm64.dmg**.
2. Drag **Companion** into **Applications**, then open it from Applications.
3. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, close the warning. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the Companion message, choose **Open Anyway**, and confirm. This creates an exception for this app; do not turn off Mac security globally. If that option is unavailable, or macOS reports damage or malware, stop and ask your beta inviter for help.
4. Choose **Enable microphone** and answer the macOS prompt, or choose **Not now** to type instead. Click the plant for **Tasks**, **Breathe** and **Settings**.

Add a reminder two minutes from now, keep your Mac awake, and check the alert. Try a one-minute breathing session with voice and music. Type or dictate an answer when the plant tells a joke.

Read `Companion-Beta-Guide.html` and `Companion-Privacy.html`, also attached below. These open locally in your browser and do not upload anything.

## Known beta limitations

- Reminders require the app to be running and the Mac awake for an on-time alert. Missed reminders are recovered when it resumes. Do not use this beta for critical alarms.
- English dictation can mishear words. Review the text before saving; the first use may take a moment to load.
- Google Calendar is optional. If Google's project is in testing mode, your Google account needs an invitation and may need to reconnect after seven days. Sign-in has not yet been independently verified on a fresh tester account for this release.
- Some secondary animations, including the meal picnic, use an earlier growth-stage appearance. The small animation Showcase remains during beta.
- Automated tests passed using isolated data and bundled models on the development Mac. Installation warnings, real microphone permissions, different Macs and extended sleep/wake behavior still need tester confirmation. This is an early test release, not a fully certified product.

## Updates and help

Updates are manual. Quit Companion, download the next beta, then replace Companion in Applications. Keep the same app/data folder and do not choose Delete all while updating; saved tasks and settings are retained. Ask before downgrading.

If something fails, quit and reopen the app. Then reply to your beta inviter with version **0.1.1-beta.1**, Mac model, macOS version and steps to reproduce. Remove private task or Calendar details from screenshots. Do not send recordings, keys or the app-data folder. No automatic diagnostics or activity tracking are uploaded.
