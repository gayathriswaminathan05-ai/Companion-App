Privacy, in plain words

A little friend.
No hidden sharing.

Your plant is here for conversation, tasks and care breaks. Chat sends information to an AI service. Your whole life on this Mac does not come along with it.

Beta privacy explanation · Updated 17 October 2026

When you chat

Before your first conversation, the app explains sharing and asks you to choose Start chatting. Only sending a message starts an AI request. Opening chat or typing a draft does not.

The request includes up to 16 recent chat messages, including the message you just sent, plus the plant's instructions and local date/time for reminders. Your task list, saved notes, profile name, growth history and fetched calendar events are not automatically added. Details you put in a chat message are part of that conversation.

Chat is powered by OpenAI. Beta chat passes through our server to OpenAI. With a personal OpenAI API key, it goes directly to OpenAI. Research answers may send search terms drawn from your conversation to search services.

What stays on your Mac

Tasks, care settings, growth, saved notes and a copy of your chat history are stored locally. Voice recognition and speaking run on your Mac in this beta. We do not upload microphone recordings for transcription or speech generation.

Google Calendar is a separate, optional connection. It contacts Google for your calendar; fetched event details stay in memory and are not added to AI prompts. Creating a Google event sends the details you explicitly confirm to Google. Gmail alerts are disabled.

There are no added analytics or advertising trackers. The app does not automatically send us your conversations, task contents, recordings or screenshots. If you share a screenshot for support, remove private details first.

Our server and the AI provider

Our chat-server code does not log conversation contents. It keeps a device identifier and daily usage counters to limit beta use. Hosting and AI services necessarily process connection information, such as IP addresses, to handle requests.

For OpenAI requests, we disable saved response retrieval with store: false. This does not mean zero retention. Providers may retain information for safety, security and other purposes under their policies. We do not claim approved Zero Data Retention for this beta.

See OpenAI's API data controls for its retention terms. The beta's provider and account settings must be verified before invitations go out.

Deletion you can understand

  • Delete chat history removes local messages and the local summary, and stops the current reply. Saved notes stay.
  • Forget a saved note removes that note locally. It does not erase the conversation in which you originally mentioned it.
  • Delete all removes local tasks, chats, notes, settings, personal API keys and Google connection credentials, and disables start at login.

Local deletion cannot recall requests already sent or delete records held by a provider. It does not delete events from your Google Calendar. Mac backups can retain older copies. Removing the app alone is not the same as deleting its local data.
