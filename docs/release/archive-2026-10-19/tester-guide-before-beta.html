Companion Beta Guide

Companion beta

Hi, I'm Blob. I'll keep you company while you work.

A tiny plant that lives on your Mac desktop. I hold your tasks, nudge you to stretch and sip water, chat when you need a friend, and grow a little every time you look after yourself.

Install in two minutes

Download: use the link Gayathri shared with you (file name Companion-0.1.0-arm64.dmg). Needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer).
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Companion into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Companion from Applications. Your Mac will say it can't check the app. That's expected for a beta that isn't on the App Store yet. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Companion, then confirm with your password. You only do this once.
  4. On first open, choose Enable microphone, then allow access in the macOS prompt to dictate tasks and answer jokes. Or choose Not now and use typing. I only record while you hold the mic; transcription happens on your Mac.

How to hang out with me

  • Click meOpens chat, tasks and settings.
  • Drag mePut me anywhere on your screen. I'll remember the spot.
  • TasksType or say “call amma at 6pm” and I'll remind you at 6.
  • ChatTell me about your day, ask me anything, or just vent.
  • Hold the micPress and hold to talk, let go when you're done. Review the words before saving a task or submitting a joke answer.
  • Answer my jokesType or dictate your guess, then tap Answer. Tap What? to skip to the punchline. Your guesses are not saved.
  • Watch me growStretch and water breaks make me bloom. I start fresh each morning.

Too chatty? Settings lets you turn off my voice, sounds, jokes and reminders. The moon button tucks me away for a nap.

Optional: connect Google

Connect Google in Settings so I can ping you before meetings, book meetings when you ask in chat, and tell you about new emails. Before you connect, send Gayathri the Gmail address you'll use, because Google only lets invited testers in during the beta.

Google will warn that the app isn't verified yet. Click Continue. During the beta Google may ask you to reconnect about once a week.

Your privacy, in plain words

  • Your tasks, chats, memories and settings live only on your Mac.
  • Chat uses OpenAI. Our server does not log conversations; OpenAI retention policies still apply.
  • Your voice is turned into text on your Mac and never leaves it.
  • When you're online, the words I say out loud are turned into speech by Google, through our private server, and aren't stored. Meeting titles and email senders are always spoken on your Mac instead.
  • If you connect Google, I see meeting times and email senders and subjects for a moment to remind you. I never save them, never read email bodies, and never pass them to the AI.
  • “Match what's behind me” is off unless you turn it on. When on, I only check how bright your screen is around me.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Settings → Privacy → Delete all wipes everything instantly.
