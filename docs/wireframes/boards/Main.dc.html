Your privacy, in plain words

Built to stay on your Mac.

  • Tasks, chats, memories and settings live only on your Mac.
  • Chat messages go to Claude only to write a reply. Not stored by us, not used for training.
  • Your voice turns into text on your Mac and never leaves it.
  • It notices when you step away from the keyboard, never what you type.
  • Google is optional. Meeting titles and email senders are never saved or sent to the AI.
  • No ads, no tracking. Settings → Delete everything wipes it all.
