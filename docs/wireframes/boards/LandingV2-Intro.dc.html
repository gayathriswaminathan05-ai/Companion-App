Auto-scroll rules (so it never feels pushy)

  • Auto-scrolls only if the visitor hasn't touched anything for ~5 seconds after the intro.
  • Any scroll, drag or tap cancels it, and the plant just waits.
  • People who turned on "reduce motion" get no auto-scroll, just the "See my day" button.
  • It only happens once per visit.

Built from things we already have

  • The plant = the real cut-out animations from the app (wave, water, bell, champagne, sleepy).
  • Its voice = the same on-device voice as the app. Chimes = the app's real sounds.
  • Desk objects = small cut-out images (generated once, cheap) on a grid-paper desk.
  • Videos = the trailer shots, cut per use case.
