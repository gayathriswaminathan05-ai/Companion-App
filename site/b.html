Your privacy, in plain words

Built to stay on your Mac.

  • Tasks, saved memories and settings stay on your Mac. Your chats, your choice.
  • When you send a message, recent chat (up to 16 messages, including your new message) go through our server to the AI. We do not log conversations.
  • Voice recognition and speaking run on your Mac. Sending dictated chat shares its text with the AI.
  • I notice when you step away from the keyboard, never what you type.
  • Google Calendar is optional. Fetched calendar events are not saved or sent to the AI.
  • No ads, no tracking. Settings → Delete all removes local app data and personal keys, not provider records.

Only messages you send in chat start an AI request. AI providers may retain data under their policies. Privacy details and deletion.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Open the download and drag Companion into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. Your Mac will say it can't check the app yet (we're a tiny beta, not on the App Store). Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. I'll pop up in the corner of your screen.
