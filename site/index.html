Download for Mac

Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer) · macOS 12 or later

Install in 3 steps

  1. Open the download and drag Companion into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. Your Mac will say it can't check the app yet (we're a tiny beta, not on the App Store). Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. I'll pop up in the corner of your screen.

Private by design

  • Your voice is turned into text on your Mac
  • Only our chats go to the AI, and we never log them
  • Calendar and email stay in memory, never saved
  • No ads, no tracking
  • Delete everything anytime, right in Settings
