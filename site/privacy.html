Privacy, in plain words

A little friend.
Your day stays yours.

This beta has tasks, reminders, guided breathing and jokes. AI chat and cloud voice are disabled. You do not need an AI account or API key.

Companion 0.1.1-beta.2 · Updated 19 October 2026

Tasks and voice stay on your Mac

Your tasks, reminder schedules, settings and growth are saved on your Mac. Dictation and the plant’s speaking voice run on your Mac using bundled models. Microphone recordings are not uploaded or saved as audio files by the app.

The microphone listens while you hold its button. You can review and edit dictated words before saving a task or answering a joke. Joke guesses disappear when their bubble closes. Microphone permission is optional; typing still works if you decline.

Guided meditation voice and music are bundled recordings. There are no AI chat or cloud speech requests in this beta, including when an older installation has a saved AI key.

Optional Google Calendar

Connecting Calendar opens Google sign-in and contacts Google to read upcoming events. New connections request read-only access to events. This beta does not create Google events or access Gmail.

Fetched event titles and times stay in memory for alerts, rather than being saved in the app’s task database. Calendar alerts are spoken on your Mac. Connection credentials are saved locally so you can stay connected. Older connections may retain permissions previously granted; you can review or revoke them in your Google Account.

You can disconnect Google in Settings. Your local tasks and breathing sessions do not need a Google connection. Google processes sign-in and Calendar requests under its own policies.

Desktop permissions and sharing

Optional “Match what’s behind me” uses screen access to estimate surrounding brightness locally. It is off unless you enable it. No captured screen image is uploaded or saved by this feature.

If call hiding is enabled, the app checks for supported calls locally, including Google Meet tabs with macOS automation permission. This is used to hide the plant during calls, not to record your browsing.

No advertising trackers, analytics or automatic bug-report uploads are added. Download hosting services receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, when you download the app. That does not give them your saved tasks.

Local storage and deletion

App data uses files restricted to your Mac account; it is not separately encrypted by the app. Mac account security, disk encryption and backups matter.

Settings → Privacy → Delete all removes the app’s local tasks, settings, old chat history and saved notes, personal AI keys and Google connection credentials, and turns off start at login. If deletion fails, the app reports it. Old chat history and notes can also be deleted separately when present.

Removing Companion from Applications alone does not remove its saved data. Delete all first if that is what you want. Deletion does not erase Mac backups, Google Calendar events or information sent to an AI service by an older version.

If something goes wrong

Quit and reopen Companion. For help, reply to the person who sent your beta invitation. Include the app version, your Mac/macOS version and what you clicked. Share screenshots only after removing private task and Calendar details. Do not send recordings, keys or your app data folder.
